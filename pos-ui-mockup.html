Design notes

A calmer, warmer counter: deep green for the brand and primary actions, cream for the workspace, white cards for the menu. The menu is the largest surface; the current order stays pinned on the right.

Search and category chips handle a growing menu without hiding items. The cart header carries the clear-order action; each line has its own stepper and remove control.

The VAT line is configurable. This sample shows it on (12%, inclusive); the design spec ships with tax disabled by default.

Five owner-selectable themes — Forest, Espresso, Ocean, Plum, and Slate — change only the brand surface. Danger, success, and warning colors stay fixed. Open the Settings tab and tap a theme to preview it live.
