Design notes

Built around the counter, not a dashboard. The menu is the largest target; the cart is always visible; the charge button is the only red primary action on the screen.

Body text is 14–16px, menu cards are 126px tall, and every tap target is at least 44×44px. Status never relies on color alone — each badge has a dot and a label.

The tab bar is for this mockup only. In the real app, navigation is the left rail with POS, Shift, and History.
